## Built for ambition

iThingy Labs is a digital product studio for founders, creators, and businesses ready to move beyond ordinary.

We combine strategy, distinctive design, and reliable engineering to create products that look exceptional, feel effortless, and grow with the people behind them.

## What we build

- **Premium websites** — fast, memorable experiences designed to turn attention into action.
- **Web platforms & SaaS** — scalable products built around real users and business goals.
- **Mobile applications** — intuitive digital experiences that stay close to your customers.
- **CRM & internal systems** — clear workflows, automation, and control without unnecessary complexity.
- **AI-powered solutions** — assistants, knowledge systems, semantic search, and intelligent product features.
- **Cloud & DevOps** — reliable infrastructure, automated delivery, observability, and room to scale.

## AI, automation & integrations

We integrate AI where it removes friction, accelerates decisions, or creates a genuinely better product — not as a decorative feature.

- **AI assistants & copilots** — customer support, lead qualification, internal operations, and product guidance.
- **RAG & private knowledge systems** — source-grounded answers, vector search, embeddings, and searchable company knowledge.
- **Document & data intelligence** — classification, extraction, summarization, enrichment, and semantic discovery.
- **Workflow automation** — connected CRM, forms, messaging, webhooks, external services, and event-driven business processes.
- **Cloud and local models** — integrations with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and private models through Ollama.
- **AI-native products** — intelligent features embedded directly into web apps, mobile apps, admin panels, analytics, and OSINT workflows.

Our integration toolkit includes `OpenAI API`, `Claude`, `Gemini`, `Ollama`, `Qdrant`, `Dify`, `n8n`, `Telegram`, third-party APIs, webhooks, and custom business systems.

## How we work

`Discover` → `Design` → `Build` → `Launch` → `Evolve`

Every project starts with the problem, not the technology. We define what matters, shape the right experience, build it properly, and keep improving after launch.

## Our standard

- Design with a point of view
- Engineering prepared for growth
- Clear communication and transparent delivery
- Business value over decorative complexity
- Products we are proud to put our name on
